# Interview prep

## Inputs
`<app>/` (JD, fit analysis, CV sent, previous rounds), `profile/achievements.md`,
web research on company and interviewers (public professional info only).

## Deliverable — a new round section in `<app>/interview-notes.md`

1. **Brief (≤ 10 bullets):** what the company does, size, recent news, team / product
   the role sits in, interviewers' roles. Cite sources.
2. **Likely questions (10–15)**, grouped: motivation · role competence · behavioural ·
   situational · logistics (availability, salary, notice). For each: the `A-xx` story to
   use + a 2-line answer skeleton. Highlight the 3 hardest (gaps from fit analysis).
3. **Salary answer** consistent with `positioning.md` (a range, with the floor in mind).
4. **Questions to ask them (5–7)** — specific to the role, not answerable from the website.
5. **Logistics:** time, place / link, who, format, what to have ready.

## Practice mode
On "let's practise": ask one question at a time, wait for the answer, then give
feedback on structure (STAR), specificity, length, and one improvement. Keep score
across the session; finish with the top 3 things to fix.

## Debrief
After the interview, ask 5 quick questions (what they asked, what landed, what didn't,
signals, next step), write them under *Debrief*, update `PIPELINE.md` and `STATE.md`,
and add any new story to `achievements.md`.
